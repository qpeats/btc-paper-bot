BTC Paper Bot.

Press Run to replay the last 24 hours of Bitcoin prices, minute by minute, with a $10,000 paper account. No real money is touched and no account is needed.

1,440 one-minute candles Start: $10,000 Fee 0.1% per side Take-profit 0.3% Add every −0.5%, max 6

What happens when you press Run

  1. The bot downloads the 1,440 closed one-minute candles for the 24 hours before now from Binance (BTC/USDT spot).
  2. It makes four $10 round trips first, then buys 1/6 of the account and adds another sixth each time the price falls 0.5%, up to six.
  3. It sells everything once the price reaches 0.3% above the average cost, and stops after five trades if it is more than $0.50 ahead.
  4. You get every trade, the final balance, a buy-and-hold comparison, and the three checks: at least 5 trades, ending above $10,000, holding 0 BTC.
